# Welcome to the Ocean Climate Connections (OCC) Research Group at Temple University!
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4faef05a-8bb1-42f5-8ab8-02da082506f0)

Research within the OCC group is guided by the central goals of understanding the ocean’s role in the Earth system and climate change and reducing uncertainty in the evolution of large-scale climate dynamics under continued warming. To achieve these goals, our work leverages global observational products and fully coupled climate model simulations. This includes the design and generation of novel climate model simulations, analysis of existing model output from international climate modeling projects, and leveraging observational datasets. We collaborate with national and international teams focused on climate model development, model evaluation, and ocean dynamics.
